# GreenFootprint: Making Sustainability Accessible

## The Problem

Most carbon footprint trackers are **boring, confusing, or inaccessible**. They use technical jargon, have poor usability, and *exclude users with disabilities*.

- Existing tools were **not accessible** to screen reader users
- Data was presented in *confusing charts* that were hard to understand
- Users felt **overwhelmed** rather than empowered
- No *personalized recommendations* for reducing impact

## My Approach

I followed a **user-centered design process** with accessibility at the *center* of every decision:

1. **Research:** Interviewed 10 people about their environmental concerns and *accessibility needs*
2. **Planning:** Created *user personas* and **accessibility requirements**
3. **Design:** Wireframed and prototyped with *Figma*, testing with screen readers
4. **Implementation:** Built with *semantic HTML5*, CSS3, and JavaScript
5. **Testing:** Validated with *W3C Validator*, NVDA, VoiceOver, and JAWS

### Key Features

- **Semantic HTML** — Every element has meaning
- **ARIA labels** — For screen reader navigation
- **Keyboard support** — Full functionality without a mouse
- **Color contrast** — WCAG 2.1 AA compliant
- **Clear data** — Visualizations that everyone can understand

## Challenges I Faced

### Making Data Accessible

How do you make **complex environmental data** understandable for *everyone*?

**Solution:** I used *simple charts*, clear language, and **multiple formats** (visual, text, and audio descriptions).

### Screen Reader Compatibility

Charts are **inherently visual** — how do you make them work for *blind users*?

**Solution:** I added *text alternatives*, ARIA labels, and **structured data tables** that screen readers can navigate.

### Keeping It Simple

Carbon footprint data is **complicated**. How do you *simplify* it without losing accuracy?

**Solution:** I focused on *one metric at a time*, used **friendly language**, and provided *actionable recommendations*.

## Results

- **100% W3C-valid** HTML across all pages
- **WCAG 2.1 AA** compliance verified
- Tested successfully with *NVDA*, *VoiceOver*, and *JAWS*
- **40%** of users reported *changing their behavior* after using the app

> "GreenFootprint is the first carbon tracker I've actually enjoyed using. The charts are clear, the recommendations are helpful, and it works with my screen reader!" — *Test user with visual impairment*
